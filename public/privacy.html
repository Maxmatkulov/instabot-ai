Privacy Policy

InstaBot AI · last updated October 1, 2026

InstaBot AI automatically replies to comments left under our own Instagram account and directs users to our Telegram bot.

Data we receive

  • Instagram: comment text, comment ID, and the commenter's Instagram-scoped ID and username; Direct message text and sender ID.
  • Telegram: user ID, first name, username, and messages sent to the bot.

How we use it

Only to detect a keyword, send a one-time private reply to the commenter, verify a Telegram channel subscription, deliver the requested material, and answer questions in the bot's AI assistant. AI questions are sent to an AI provider (Groq or Anthropic) solely to generate a reply.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed only while handling a request and is not stored in a permanent database. We do not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Data deletion

To request deletion, message @mmn0300 on Telegram or send "DELETE" via Instagram Direct. Requests are completed within 7 days. You can revoke the app's access in your Instagram settings at any time.
